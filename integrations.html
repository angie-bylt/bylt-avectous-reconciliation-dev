How these numbers are worked out
Internal reference. The same notes are on the Method sheet of the export.
Direction is different for the two halves, and it matters
  1. Order sync runs NetSuite → Avectous. NetSuite creates the order and the queue pushes it out, so the test is: of the orders NetSuite holds, how many reached Avectous?
  2. Fulfillment sync runs Avectous → NetSuite. The warehouse physically ships, then confirms back. So the test is the reverse: of the orders Avectous shipped, how many did NetSuite record?
Checking fulfillments the other way round — taking orders NetSuite already fulfilled and asking whether Avectous agrees — reads about 99% and means nothing. An order NetSuite never fulfilled can't appear in that sample, so every real failure is excluded by construction.
Fulfilled in NetSuite, no Avectous shipment
Counted and listed separately, never inside queue health — the denominator there is what Avectous shipped, so an order Avectous has no record of cannot belong in it. These are the mirror failure and need chasing from the NetSuite end rather than Avectous's.
Cancelled orders are excluded
  1. Any order whose WMS Status is Pending Cancellation, Cancellation Confirmed or Cancellation Failed.
  2. Any order whose NetSuite Status is Closed.
  3. CX is trying to stop these, so a missing sync is not a queue fault. Excluded from every percentage and listed on their own sheet in the export.
Matching
  1. Sales orders match on PO/Check Number against Avectous OrderNumber.
  2. Transfer orders match on Document Number against the same column.
  3. Avectous puts sales and transfer orders in one export, so each NetSuite search is matched against the whole file rather than trusting OrderType.
Orders only Avectous has
Listed separately, never inside a health percentage. These are usually test orders or orders created after the NetSuite export was pulled. They are in the export so you can check.
