How every number on this tab is counted
Internal reference. This sits inside the upload panel so the dashboard itself stays clean — the same notes are on the Summary & Method sheet of Export proof, which is the version to share.
Two facts per order, and only two
  1. The day it was created — NetSuite Date.
  2. The day it shipped — Avectous RecordDate.
Shipped means Avectous shipped it
Avectous is the system that physically ships, so it decides. NetSuite is consulted only where Avectous has no record of the order at all — a small number fulfilled by hand.
The two tables answer different questions
  1. Orders in vs out — how many arrived that day against how many left the building. Throughput.
  2. By the day the order came in — of Monday's orders, how many are done by now. Progress.
They can disagree and that is useful. A slow shipping day can belong to a creation day that later cleared completely.
Closed days are shown, not hidden
A day with 200+ orders in and none out is marked "DC closed" and greyed. Dropping those rows would hide why the backlog exists — two closed weekends took 5,747 orders and shipped none.
Counting orders once
Grouped by Internal ID. Matched to Avectous on PO/Check Number for sales orders and Document Number for transfer orders, because those are what Avectous stores.
