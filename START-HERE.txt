WHAT IT FOUND
-------------
  Sales order sync            98.69%     263 missing
  Transfer order sync         99.77%       4 missing
  Sales order fulfillments    78.73%   3,126 missing
  Transfer order fulfillments 49.88%     205 missing

Orders going OUT to Avectous are fine.
Ship confirmations coming BACK are not.

Of the 3,126 missing sales-order fulfillments, only 59 shipped
on the latest Avectous day. The other 3,067 shipped earlier and
have had far longer than the 15-minute queue interval.


WHY THE DIRECTION MATTERS
-------------------------
Order sync is measured NetSuite -> Avectous.
Fulfillments are measured Avectous -> NetSuite.

Measuring fulfillments the other way -- taking orders NetSuite
already fulfilled and asking whether Avectous agrees -- gives
99.78% and is meaningless. An order NetSuite never fulfilled
cannot appear in that sample, so every real failure is excluded
by construction.

Same data, correct direction: 78.73%.
