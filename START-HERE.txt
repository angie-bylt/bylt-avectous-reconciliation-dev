WHAT CHANGED
------------
"In Avectous only" is now split into two rows, because two very
different things were in that bucket:

  Avectous test orders              14
  Created after the NetSuite pull  182

TEST ORDERS are order numbers containing "TEST":
    SHPYTEST31, SHPYTEST32, GOLIVETEST36 ...
These should not be in a production warehouse. The tab shows a
red callout for them. GOLIVETEST36 looks like a leftover from
cutover testing.

CREATED AFTER THE PULL are real Shopify orders numbered higher
than anything in your NetSuite export, dated up to 8/21. Avectous
is working them correctly -- your NetSuite file is just older.
Nothing wrong, so they are shown in grey, not red.

Both were previously counted together as one number, which hid
the test pollution behind a timing artefact.


THE EXPORT
----------
The "In Avectous Only" sheet has two new columns:

    Found in   -- Order download or Shipments
    Kind       -- Avectous test order / Created after the NetSuite pull

Filter Kind to "Avectous test order" and that is the list to
send Avectous asking them to clear it.
