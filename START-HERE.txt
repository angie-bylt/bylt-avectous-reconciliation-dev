WHAT CHANGED FROM UPDATE 3
--------------------------
The "Show how this is counted" links are gone from the dashboard.
It now reads as a finished report, not a worked example.

The counting rules moved to two places instead:

1. FOR YOU -- inside the collapsed "Update these numbers" panel
   at the bottom of the tab. Open it and scroll past the file
   pickers. Every rule is there, plus a live readout of which
   columns were detected on the last run.

2. FOR SHARING -- the Summary & Method sheet inside Export proof.
   That is the version to hand to anyone who asks how a number
   was arrived at.

Nothing else changed. Same sections, same figures.
