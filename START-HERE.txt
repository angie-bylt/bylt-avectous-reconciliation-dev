WHAT'S NEW
----------
"How far back is the warehouse caught up?" -- one section for
sales orders, one for transfer orders. Sits above the channel
tables.

One row per day. Each row shows what share of the orders due
that day have shipped, plus how many are still open.

Three headline numbers:
    caught up through    8/10
    where it falls off   8/17  (drops 44.4 points in a day)
    orders open since    12,189

A dashed line marks where it stops being clean. A solid red
line marks the cliff. Both are calculated, not typed in.


WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT FROM "SHIPPED PER DAY"
--------------------------------------------
They answer opposite questions and they disagree:

  Shipped per day says the warehouse got 2,136 orders out
  the door on 8/19 -- their best day.

  This new section says orders DUE on 8/19 are 0.1% shipped.

Both are true. The warehouse is working flat out on old
orders and never reaching the new ones. Throughput alone
would have said everything was fine.


GROUPING
--------
By Ship Date, never earlier than the order date.

Ship Date, because a prebook placed in March with an August
ship date is due in August -- not five months late. That alone
moves 38 orders out of March.

Floored at the order date, because a replacement order
inherits the original's ship date. Without the floor, an order
CX creates on the 21st carrying a ship date of the 8th would
look two weeks late the instant it exists.
